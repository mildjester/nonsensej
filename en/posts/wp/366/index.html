Closing the Books for a Dormant Company with MF Cloud Accounting

posted: 2017-02-07

法人事務

Closing the fiscal year for a dormant company without hiring a tax accountant.
I use the free plan of MF Cloud Accounting.

(1) Enter the required information under Settings > Business.

(2) Under Settings > Opening Balances, enter the cash and deposit amounts carried over from last year.

(3) You probably received some bank interest, so record a transfer slip for that amount.

(4) Export the following as PDF:
“Closing & Filing Menu > Financial Statements”
“Accounting Books Menu > Cash and Deposit Book”
“Accounting Books Menu > General Ledger”

(5) File the final tax return via e-Tax.
Detailed steps are here

If the tax office doesn’t contact you after this, you’re done.
