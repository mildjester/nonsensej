When Google Tag Manager Doesn't Fire

posted: 2018-11-26

GoogleService

This is how to investigate when you’ve set up triggers with Google Tag Manager
but they aren’t being tracked in Google Analytics as expected.

First, switch to “Preview mode” in the Google Tag Manager console.
Just click “Preview” next to the Publish button.

Once in preview mode, open the page you want to test in the same browser.
The Google Tag Manager console will be displayed.
When you click on the page, you can see click events being generated.

If you open “Variables” or “Data Layer” in the console,
you can see what kind of trigger fired.

Check whether the trigger fired on the click (ID or Class) you confirmed here
matches the trigger configured in Google Tag Manager.

A Likely Mistake

In Google Tag Manager, the innermost element becomes the trigger for an event.
Note that this differs from JavaScript’s onClick.

For example, with the following source code, clicking sample.png
does not fire a click event for id=“fooo”.

<div id="wrapper">  
  <div id="fooo">  
    <img src="./sample.png" />  
  </div>  
</div>
