[Mac] Connecting to a VPN with FortiClient

posted: 2017-10-13

Mac

■Environment

macOS Sierra 10.12.6
FortiClient 5.6.0.703

■Downloading & installing

Download the installer from the official site.
http://www.forticlient.com/downloads

Double-click the downloaded dmg file to launch the installer,
then just follow the installer to complete the installation.

■VPN settings

Launch FortiClient and click “Remote Access > gear icon > Add a new connection”.

Configure the connection and click the “Add” button.

Then enter your password and click the “Connect” button to connect.

Once configured, you can connect and disconnect from the FortiClient icon in the menu bar at the top right.

*The FortiClient icon is a shield with a lock inside when connected to the VPN, and a square inside when disconnected.
