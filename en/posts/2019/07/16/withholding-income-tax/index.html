Filing Withholding Income Tax via e-Tax

posted: 2019-07-16

法人事務

I filed withholding income tax via e-Tax, so here are my notes on the steps.
This assumes you have enough basic knowledge to file withholding income tax on paper at the tax office.

Creating the Application Form

From the e-Tax left menu, open Create (作成) > Returns/Applications (申告・申請等) and click New (新規作成).

Next, select the following and click “Next” (次へ).

  • Type of procedure: Applications/Notifications (申請・届出)
  • Tax item: Withholding income tax (源泉所得税)

Next, check the following and click “Next”.

  • Statement of tax collected (徴収高計算書関係)
    • Statement of income tax collected on employment income, retirement income, etc. (給与所得・退職所得等の所得税徴収高計算書) (*)
      • Statement of income tax collected on employment income, retirement income, etc. (給与所得・退職所得等の所得税徴収高計算書) (*)

*Companies that pay income tax monthly choose “General” (一般); companies that pay half a year’s worth together choose “Special payment deadline” (納期特例分)

A screen appears for naming the return/application; give it a name you’ll recognize and click “OK”.
(e.g. “XXXX年度〇期_源泉所得税”)

Next, the basic information screen appears; enter or confirm the following and click “OK”.

  • Tax office: the tax office you’re filing with (the municipality where your company is located)
  • Submission date: today’s date
  • Corporate name (kana): your company name in kana
  • Corporate name (required): your corporate name
  • Place of tax payment: your company’s address and phone number
  • Representative name (kana):
  • Representative name (required):
  • Representative: the representative’s address and phone number
  • Financial institution: the company’s bank account

A list of forms opens; double-click the form you selected earlier to open it.
You can then edit the form’s contents, so fill it in.
The text is small and hard to read, but right-clicking brings up a zoom menu.

Basically you just enter “for what period”, “to how many people”, “how much was paid”, and “how much was withheld”.
(Be careful: you also need to enter “for what period” in the “payment period category” (納期等の区分) field on the far right (the name may vary).)

The official explanation is here.
http://www.e-tax.nta.go.jp/tetsuzuki/tetsuzuki4_4.htm (Japanese)

When you’ve finished filling it in, click “Finish creating” (作成完了).
If you need a copy, print it here.

Submitting the Application

From the e-Tax left menu, open “To list of sendable items” (送信可能一覧へ) > “Send” (送信).

The form you just created is displayed; select it and click “Send”.
If there are problems, errors are shown; correct the indicated parts and send again.

Paying the Withholding Income Tax

When the submission completes, a receipt confirmation message arrives in your message box,
and you can make the payment from the button below that message.

If paying online,
credit card payments incur a fee,
so internet banking is better.

That completes filing the withholding income tax.
