Making a Lost Child Tag with Google Forms

posted: 2021-01-25

GoogleService

Now that my kids can run around freely, the risk of them getting lost in shopping centers and amusement facilities has increased, so I considered making lost child tags.
I set the following requirements for the tag.

  • Attach it to the child’s belongings, etc.
  • Let the person who finds the child contact the parent
  • I don’t want to write my contact information directly on the tag
  • I want to be able to change it immediately if the contact information leaks to someone malicious
  • I don’t want to create a disposable email address because managing it is a hassle

That’s where Google Forms comes in.
I’ll make the lost child tag with the following flow.

(1) Create a contact form with Google Forms.
(2) Turn the URL of the created Google Form into a QR code.
(3) Make an accessory or similar item containing the QR code.

This time I’m making it as a lost child tag, but if you turn the QR code into a sticker and put it on your smartphone or travel bag,
a good-hearted person who finds it might contact you if you lose it.

Prerequisites

You already have a Google account (Gmail).

Detailed steps

Creating the Google Form

First, create a contact form with Google Forms.
https://www.google.com/intl/ja_jp/forms/about/

The form only needs enough fields to get in touch with the person contacting you.

Change the form settings.
Click the gear icon at the top right to open the settings popup.

First, on the General tab, uncheck basically everything.

On the Presentation tab, you can change the message shown to the person who contacted you under “Confirmation message”.

This isn’t a quiz form, so leave the Quizzes tab alone.

Next, set up how you receive the contact.
Select the Responses tab and click the icon with vertically stacked dots.

Check “Get email notifications for new responses”.
Now you’ll receive an email whenever someone submits the Google Form.
If you want to keep a history of contacts in a spreadsheet, set it under “Select response destination”.

The form is now complete.
Get the form URL from “Send” at the top right.

Creating the QR code

Use a QR code generation service to turn the Google Form URL into a QR code.
Free services include the following.
https://qr.quel.jp/ (Japanese)

Making the accessory

The generated QR code can be used in many ways.
You could make it a sticker or an accessory.

This time I made an accessory.
I used the following service.
https://originalprint.jp/ (Japanese)

You can design the accessory yourself.
You can make something like this.

Scan the QR code with a smartphone, open the Google Form, and try submitting contact information.
If you receive an email at your Gmail address saying the Google Form was submitted, it worked.
(There is a time lag of just under a minute from form submission to email arrival)

Attach this to your child’s bag.
If the Google Form URL leaks to someone malicious, just recreate the Google Form.
