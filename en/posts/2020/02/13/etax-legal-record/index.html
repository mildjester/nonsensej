Filing Statutory Reports with e-Tax

posted: 2020-02-13

法人事務

My fiscal year ended and I filed statutory reports (法定調書) with e-Tax, so here are my notes on the steps.
The calculations were done in Money Forward Payroll, so they’re omitted here.

Creating the Application

From the e-Tax left menu, open Create > Returns/Applications (作成＞申告・申請等) and click New (新規作成).

Next, select the following and click “Next”.
If “Statutory reports” (法定調書関係) isn’t among the tax item options, install “法定調書” from the install menu shown when e-Tax starts.

  • Procedure type: Application/Notification (申請・届出)
  • Tax item: Statutory reports (法定調書関係)

Next, check the following two and click “Next”.

  • Statutory reports on withholding for employment income (and summary table) (給与所得の源泉徴収等の法定調書（及び同合計表）)
    • Withholding slip for employment income (給与所得の源泉徴収票)
    • Summary table of statutory reports such as withholding slips for employment income (給与所得の源泉徴収票等の法定調書合計表)

A screen for naming the return/application appears; give it a name you’ll recognize and click “OK”.
(e.g. “XXXX年度_法定調書”)

Next, the basic information screen appears; enter or confirm the following and click “OK”.

  • Tax office: the tax office you’re filing with (the municipality where your company is located)
  • Submission date: today’s date
  • Corporation name (kana): your company name in kana
  • Corporation name (required): your corporation name
  • Tax payment location: your company’s address and phone number
  • Representative name (kana):
  • Representative name (required):
  • Representative: the representative’s address and phone number
  • Financial institution: the company’s bank account

The form list opens; double-click “Withholding slip for employment income” (給与所得の源泉徴収票) selected earlier to open it.
You’ll be asked for the submission category; select “New submission” (新規分提出), enter the target year, and click “OK”.

You can now edit the contents of the form, so fill it in.
(I downloaded the forms from Money Forward Payroll and referred to them for the input.)
The text is small and hard to read, but right-clicking shows a zoom menu.

When you’re done, click “Creation complete” (作成完了).
If you need a copy, print it here.

Back in the form list, double-click “Summary table of statutory reports such as withholding slips for employment income” (給与所得の源泉徴収票等の法定調書合計表) to open it.
Fill it in the same way and click “Creation complete”.

One caveat when transcribing forms downloaded from Money Forward Payroll:
only the submission category for salary needs to be changed from 30 (paper) to 14 (electronic).

Signing the Application

Open “Signable list” > “Digital signature” (署名可能一覧へ＞電子署名) in the e-Tax left menu.
The data you created is shown; sign it.

Sending the Application

Open “Sendable list” > “Send” (送信可能一覧へ＞送信) in the e-Tax left menu.

The forms you created are shown; select them and click “Send”.
If there are any problems, errors are displayed; correct the indicated parts and send again.

That completes the statutory report filing.
